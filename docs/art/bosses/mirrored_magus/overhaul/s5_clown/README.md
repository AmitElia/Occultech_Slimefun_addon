# The Mirrored Magus redesign, session 5: the horror clown's face

The user rejected all three masks of the study ([../s5_masks](../s5_masks/README.md)): "much more pronounced,
detailed, and full of depth... focus on the face. It should be a horror clown face."

So he has no mask: this is his own face, a horror clown's, sculpted in depth out from the illager's head
(`magus_s2.mask_clown`, 49 more cubes) and painted.

![The horror clown's face: front, three-quarter, side, from a player's eye, and on the whole figure](clown.png)

**Sculpted:**
- **The brow:** heavy, pinched down in the middle into a glare.
- **The eyes:** deep sockets behind the brow, with his cold eyes glinting far back inside them (pushed back to the face
  itself, so they sit 2 to 3 texels deep).
- **The cheeks:** high cheekbones, with cheeks pushing up into the grin.
- **The nose:** a bulbous red clown's nose.
- **The grin:** huge, stretched up into his cheeks. Thick lips built cube by cube round an open dark mouth, rows of
  uneven teeth set back behind the lips, and longer fangs at the corners.
- **The chin:** long and pointed.
- **The hair:** wild tufts sticking out from under his hat.

**Painted:**
- **The greasepaint:** white and cracked, wrapping round his cheeks onto the sides of his head.
- **Black:** inside the sockets and under the brow, with a black diamond running down from each eye.
- **The mouth:** the inside dark red-black, the teeth yellowed and darker at the gums.
- **The lips:** lipstick smeared past them, and red drips running from the corners of the grin down to his chin.
- **The nose:** glossy red, with a highlight and a crack.

Regenerate with `python tools/art/bosses/magus_clown.py`.
