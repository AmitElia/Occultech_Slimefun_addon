## What changed

| Part | Take 2 |
|---|---|
| The steppe helm | No bowl. An iron band hugs the head from the brow to the crown, built as an octagon (face plates flush, corner plates just over the head's corners) with a gold rim at the brow, so it reads round on the square head. From it an eight-sided cone rises in four tiers to a spike (an ogee: steep at the foot, curving in near the top), ribbed in gold like a spangenhelm. A mail aventail hangs from the band over the nape, the cheeks' back half and the shoulders; the red horsehair plume falls from the spike. The face stays bare. |
| The skeleton horse | A war-horse frame (the destrier's size: 31 wide, a 32-deep ribcage, 4.3 blocks at the withers) and heavier bones (x1.5): thick upper leg bones tapering to slimmer cannons, knobby joints, a wider and deeper ribcage, a thick neck, a keel of breastbone jutting forward between the shoulders, big shoulder blades and pelvis, a heavier jaw and cheekbones on the vanilla 2x skull. |
| The Vanguard | A's charger, A's armour and lance, C's horned armet (unchanged from take 1). |
