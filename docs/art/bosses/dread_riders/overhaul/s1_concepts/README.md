# Dread Riders, session 1: concepts (fresh start, 2026-11-20)

Script: `tools/art/bosses/dread_concepts.py` (run from the repository root; `A B C` for some takes, `vanguard` /
`outrider` for one rider, `quick` for a fast pass, `bare` for the horses alone). Side by side: [compare.png](compare.png).
The horses without dressing (the anatomy check): [bare.png](bare.png).

## The brief

- **The warhorses are the main focus, and they are large.** The destrier stands 4.3 blocks at the withers, the courser
  3.9. Real horse proportions: legs as long as the body is deep, a long neck carried high, the body lofted along a
  horse's profile (breast, withers, the dip of the back, a croup sloping to the tail, the belly rising to the flank).
- **Vanilla's horse head at 2x** on both (cranium 12x10x14, muzzle 8x10x10, ears 4x6x2), with a jowl so it reads as a
  horse's wedge, not a chess knight. The destrier is a zombie horse, the courser a skeleton horse.
- **The riders keep vanilla's heads at 2x**, faces bare: every helmet is a bowl worn on the head that stops at the brow
  (eyes, nose and mouth stay visible), plus what makes it a helmet for its role.
  - **The Vanguard** (zombie): a dark lancer in netherite-dark plate, crimson accents.
  - **The Outrider** (skeleton): a cavalry archer-mage. Its storm snipe ends in lightning, so its magic is hollow-cyan
    storm light.
- Tier 3: dark plate, old bone, hollow cyan and crimson. Nothing here comes from the earlier Dread Riders rounds.

Each sheet shows both riders at rest and in their moment (the Vanguard's charge, the Outrider's snipe with a hoof
raised): three-quarter, side silhouette, a player's eye, and a close-up of the rider's head. A player stands next to
the three-quarter views for scale. Pieces mix freely between takes: say, A's charger with B's burgonet, or C's storm
steed under A's steppe archer.

## What these blockouts are not yet

- Flat colours and boxes for shape only; the chamfered masses read as facets until session 4 paints them.
- The rider sits on the saddle and the arms are posed by hand; the poses are for the silhouette, not the animation.
- The horse heads are vanilla's boxes at 2x; detail such as nostrils, the jaw line and the eye goes in the texture.
