# Dread Riders, session 1, round 2

Script: `tools/art/bosses/dread_s1b.py` (run from the repository root; `war` or `pestilence` for one rider, `quick` for a
fast pass). Sheet: [sheet_war_pestilence.png](sheet_war_pestilence.png).

Round 1 ([../s1_blockout](../s1_blockout/README.md)) was rejected on 2026-11-20:
- War's horse should be a buffed warhorse and still a zombie, like its rider;
- the spears looked goofy and the banner like cardboard;
- the skeleton horse looked like a donkey or a pony, and should be intimidating and scary.

Round 2 is one crafted design per rider instead of three generated takes.

| Part | Round 2 |
|---|---|
| War's horse | A buffed zombie destrier, 2.5 blocks at the withers. Its build: a deep chest, heavy shoulders and haunches as their own masses, a thick arched crest, feathered fetlocks over big hooves. The zombie: rotting green-grey hide, a torn flank showing ribs, a gash on the neck and haunch, the left cheek rotted through to the teeth, the left ear torn, ember eyes, a heavy ragged mane. Its plate: a chanfron with eye guards, a crinet of four lames, a curved peytral with a boss. A war saddle with a red, dagged cloth. |
| The head | The vanilla horse's head at 1.6x (its cranium and narrower muzzle, the ears), pinned ears, tilted down off the poll. |
| The lance | A tapered shaft, a leather grip, a vamplate cone of three discs guarding the hand, a socket and a slim leaf blade with a diamond section. |
| The banner | At rest, a gonfanon on a crossbar under the blade, with gold finials and a tasseled cord. It falls in five pleated panels; the lower halves sway back and end in dagged points. A red sigil and border. In the charge it streams back under the couched lance, rippling, and splits into two tails. |
| Pestilence's horse | A skeleton horse as a predator. Its frame: tall and long-legged, the neck low and the skull (vanilla's at 1.8x) thrust forward, the jaw hanging open. The withers' spines rise tall and swept back like a crest of spikes. Inside: a deep ribcage with floating ribs and the plague glowing sick green in its hollow. Rot: a dried sinew from the spine to the skull, hide hanging off the bones in rags and a scrap still on the hindquarters, mould, bile drooling from the jaw. |
| Pestilence | Vanilla skeleton's long thin limbs. A deep hood with the skull inside: a brow ridge over hollow sockets, the eyes burning green, cheekbones, the jaw hanging. A plague crown over the hood. The ribcage glows like the horse's. A rotten robe drapes down the back and over the horse. A bone recurve bow bound with sinew, and a quiver of bone-fletched arrows. |
