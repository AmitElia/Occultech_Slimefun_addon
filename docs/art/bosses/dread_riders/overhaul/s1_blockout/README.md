# Dread Riders, session 1: Pestilence and War, three takes

Script: `tools/art/bosses/dread_s1.py` (run from the repository root; `A B C` to render some takes, `quick` for a fast
pass). Side by side: [compare.png](compare.png). Plan: [../plan.md](../plan.md).

## The design (picked 2026-11-20 from five pitches)

- **Pestilence (the Outrider):** a pale skeleton on a sick white horse, with a bone bow. Eerie, and a little gross:
  ribs through holes in the hide, shelf fungus, drool, bile.
- **War (the Vanguard):** a zombie in heat-reddened plate with an ember-lit visor, on a red roan warhorse, with a
  cleaver-headed lance and a black war banner bearing a red sigil.
- **Real horse proportions:** War's horse stands 2.5 blocks at the withers and Pestilence's 2.25. War reaches about
  4.5 blocks to the top of his helm, and 7.5 to 8 to the lance's tip at rest. Pestilence is about 4.6 to 5 blocks tall.
- **Poses:** each rider at rest and in its moment: Pestilence drawing the bow (the snipe), War charging with the
  lance couched (the horse galloping, the banner streaming).

## The takes

| Take | Pestilence | Its horse | War | His horse | Sheet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, the Pale Crown and the Standard | hunched under a grave-shroud hood, a thin tarnished crown, a bow carved from a single rib | starved, its head hanging to the ground; ribs through holes in the hide, the hip bones and spine jutting, shelf fungus, drool | full plate, a great helm with an ember slit and a small crest; the banner hangs from a crossbar on his lance | barded (chanfron, crinet, peytral) under a dagged red caparison | [A](A_the_pale_crown_and_the_standard.png) |
| B, Rot and Ruin | gaunt, standing in the stirrups, a bare skull in a crown of thorns, a bigger bow made of a spine | bloated: a swollen sac hangs under the belly, split along the bottom, fungus spilling out, bile dripping | molten seams glowing in heavy plate, huge pauldrons; the banner flies from a pole strapped to his back, so it's up even in the charge | a bare red roan, its mane and tail burning | [B](B_rot_and_ruin.png) |
| C, the Plague Doctor and the Siege | a plague doctor's beak mask with sick-green lenses, a rotten wide-brimmed hat, a long coat; a censer of spores at the saddle | a skeleton at the front (ribcage, skull head, bone legs) and bloated rotting flesh behind | a spiked crown-helm; a longer lance and a bigger banner | plate barding like a siege engine: a ram spike on the chest, a spike on the brow, plated flanks and rump | [C](C_the_plague_doctor_and_the_siege.png) |

Each sheet shows both riders at rest and in their moment: three-quarter, front and side silhouettes, and a player's-eye
clay view. A player stands next to every three-quarter view for scale. The pieces mix freely between takes, for
example A's starved horse under C's plague doctor, or B's back banner on A's War.
