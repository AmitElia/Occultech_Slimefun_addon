# The Blaze Choir redesign: the plan (the Percussion Choir)

Direction (2026-11-09). It replaces the rods of vanilla's blaze with musical instruments, and takes the organ pipes from
the "Furnace Cantors" draft in [boss-visual-overhaul.md](../../../../boss-visual-overhaul.md).

## Identity

The user's brief:
- **Vanilla's floating blaze head stays**, above the body, made special: decorated, liturgical, musical.
- **The body is an instrument, not rods.** Wind chimes that can also read as organ pipes.
- **Three distinct silhouettes, three instruments:** a wind chime, a pair of cymbals, and another percussive instrument.

| Singer | Instrument | Silhouette | Headpiece | Fire |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Soprano | a wind chime of hanging organ pipes | tall and thin | a fringe of small tubes round a circlet, a single spire | white |
| Alto | two great cymbals round a tower of small ones | wide | a red cantor's cap, a small cymbal at each temple | orange |
| Bass | a great church bell, the fire its clapper | heavy | the Bell Wheel: the wheel and yoke that swing a church bell, behind and over the head | deep red |

All three wear the same small glowing singing mouth on vanilla's face.

**Vanilla ties:** the blaze head at a whole scale; the fire; the vanilla bell.

## The fight stays

Three singers orbit the altar, the shield passes every 6 s, Chorus flame circles, the survivors sing faster. Each singer
is its own model on its own carrier. Things the instruments can show, to be agreed:

- **Shield:** the shielded singer's instrument closes up (the chime's pipes draw in, the cymbals shut).
- **Fireballs:** each one leaves on a struck beat.
- **A singer falls:** a single falling note; the others burn hotter.
- **Last singer:** its instrument breaks open, exposed.

## Materials

Not repeated from other bosses: **brass and bronze**, dull and heat-stained, which must stay apart from the Archevoker's
polished gold; red cord and tassels as a small liturgical accent.
