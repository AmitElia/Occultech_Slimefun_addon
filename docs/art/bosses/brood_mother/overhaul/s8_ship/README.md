# Brood Mother redesign, session 8: shipped

The candle-laden tarantula is the real fight now. Generated by `python tools/art/bosses/brood_ship.py` (after the
session scripts).

## No flickering faces

The ship script refuses to ship a model in which two faces share a plane: they z-fight in game, the Volley neck bug.
`bosskit/checks.flicker_pairs` now groups faces by plane, so it checks her 856 cubes in about 3 seconds (it skips the
zero-area edges of flat cards). The first scan found 303 clashing pairs. All are fixed at their source:

- **Wax lumps:** a lump's two middle steps were both full width. Each step is now strictly narrower than the one it
  sits on, and the lumps are lifted apart a fraction of a texel until no two lumps' steps share a plane. Drips are
  placed from the real steps afterwards.
- **Candles:** each flame now starts where its wick ends; their crossed planes used to overlap.
- **Leg knuckles:** they are 45-degree diamonds now (free to draw). A square knuckle the size of the next segment
  shared its planes. The coxa is also a little thicker than the femur.
- **Bevelled body boxes:** their three parts each differ by a texel more on one axis.
- **Leg fur and fringe cards:** alternate cards sit on their own planes.
- **Everything else:** 51 small parts (drips, beads, puddles, a few candles) are nudged up to half a texel off the
  plane they shared, which is invisible. A resolver repeats this until nothing clashes, and caches the result.

## Who triggers what

| Server event (BroodMother.java) | Mythic skill | Model |
|---|---|---|
| spawn | `model`, `bodyrotation` on the mob | spawn clip (automatic) |
| her melee hit | the mob's `~onAttack` | `bite` |
| the pounce telegraph (1 s warning) | `BROOD_MOTHER_POUNCE_PREPARE` | `pounce`: the tell, the leap the server throws her into, the landing |
| the web telegraph (1.5 s warning) | `BROOD_MOTHER_WEB_PREPARE` | `web`: tip up, quiver, jerk as the web lands |
| egg sacs laid | `BROOD_MOTHER_EGG_SAC` | `egg_sac`, and a laying sound |
| health below 75% (new) | `BROOD_MOTHER_SNUFF_75` | `snuff`; the knee and flank flames go out, with smoke and an extinguish sound |
| health below 50% (new) | `BROOD_MOTHER_SNUFF_50` | the thorax and head flames go out |
| health below 25% (new) | `BROOD_MOTHER_SNUFF_25` | the pile goes out: only her red eyes glow |
| death | - | death clip (automatic) |

After a restart, the resumed fight sees her low health again and puts the same candles out.

**The Mythic carrier:** a spider at normal size; the model is drawn as authored, with an 8-block leg span. Its
`ob_hitbox` (5 x 2.5 x 5 blocks) covers her face, thorax and abdomen, and passes every hit on to the carrier. The
vanilla body (no Mythic) is unchanged: a spider at 2.2x scale.

**Her bite reach:** with the Mythic body the carrier is a normal-size spider, so her melee reach is a vanilla
spider's from the centre of the model. A player has to step in under her front legs to be bitten. That is fair, and
reads right with the model, but it is a change from the 2.2x vanilla spider; worth watching in the in-game review.

## Fixes found on the way

- **ClearLaggEnhanced could clear a Mythic boss:** Mythic spawns its carrier before Occultech marks it, so the clear
  guard never tagged it. `MythicBody` now tags the carrier after the setup. This was found by the self-test, because
  its first summon is the Brood Mother, which now has a Mythic body; Volley's carrier had the same gap.
- **A copied model was locked as a staff edit:** the installer treated a file identical to the bundled one, but
  without an install record, as a staff edit, so later updates to it would never install. An exact copy now counts
  as installed.
- **M2's `@trigger` telegraph is verified:** the self-test's stand-in target was a marker armour stand, which Mythic's
  targeters skip. With a living stand-in, Volley's burst skill draws its telegraph under the action target after a
  live `/mm reload`.

## Fixes from the first in-game review

- **The model came a second late:** the plain spider (and Volley's plain skeleton) showed for about a second before
  the model replaced it, because the model waited for the mob's onSpawn skill. `MythicBody` now attaches the model
  the moment the carrier spawns, and hides the vanilla body at once. The self-test still finds exactly one tracker.
- **The eyes didn't follow the head (both bosses):** BetterModel's `h` tag turns only the head bone itself;
  `hi` (head with children) turns everything hanging off it. Both shipped models now use `hi_head`, so the eyes,
  jaws, pedipalps and head candles (Brood Mother) and the eye glint and jaw (Volley) turn with the head.
- **Clunky walk, gliding, not facing the pounce:** see the
  [revised animation](../s7_animation/README.md). `BroodMother.move()` holds her still and turned to her mark
  through the pounce tell, and faces her along her flight.

## In game

1. Push the pack repository (`occultech_texture_pack_v1.0`).
2. Restart your server with the run-server script, so it gets the new jar.
3. Summon her at a tier 0 circle, or use `/occultech showcase`.

Please check:
- **The model:** she appears; the eyes and flames glow; the hair reads as fur.
- **Animation:** the walk and the attacks play on cue (pounce, web, egg sacs, bite).
- **Candles:** they go out at 75%, 50% and 25%.
- **Hitbox:** hits on her face and abdomen count.
- **Bite reach:** see the note above.
