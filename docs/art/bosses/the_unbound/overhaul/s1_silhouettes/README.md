# The Unbound redesign, session 1 step 1: posture silhouettes

The direction and the brief are in [../plan.md](../plan.md): the Horned Frenzy, no mask. Clearly a vindicator, larger
than a player with a buff upper body, bull horns, a red cloth over his eyes with one eye burning through, chains on
his limbs, a burning chest piece, silver-studded straps, and the Frenzy Cleaver at his size, bloodied.

All three postures share one design. They differ only in how he stands and carries the cleaver. Each is about
2.9 blocks to the top of his head (the player is 1.8), with the horns above that.

![Three postures, side and front, silhouette and value](silhouettes.png)

| | Posture | Read |
|---|---|---|
| A | **The Charging Bull** | Leaning far forward, head low between the shoulders, horns lowered ahead like a bull's; the cleaver swung low and ahead at his side, ready to sweep; knees bent, a wide stance. The most aggressive; the cleaver covers his chest from the side. |
| B | **The Executioner** | Upright, chest out, the cleaver resting on his right shoulder with the blade behind his back; the left fist hangs with its chain; the horns sweep up and out. The clearest torso (chest piece and straps), the most "boss on display". |
| C | **The Brute** | A gorilla's build: huge hunched shoulders, long heavy arms hanging past his knees, short legs; the cleaver dragged behind him. The widest, heaviest front. |

The red band and the burning eye (the viewer's right) are on every take, and the horn tips burn.
