# The Unbound redesign, session 1 step 3: the full blockout

The whole figure from cuboids, built on the locked head and chest ([../s1_face3](../s1_face3/README.md)) and the
take-2 silhouette ([../s1_silhouettes](../s1_silhouettes/README.md)), in his two poses: walking with the cleaver
dragged, and the cleave's wind-up with it over his shoulder.

**The figure:**
- **Size:** about 3.25 blocks to the top of his head and 3.7 to the horn tips, next to a 1.8-block player.
- **Posture:** a heavy upper body (a deep chest, a broad back and a hump of traps behind the low head) leaning
  12 degrees forward over his hips, so it sits over his legs; the head comes forward under the hump. It started at
  20 degrees, which tipped him over; the back mass is what balances it.
- **Build:** bare grey skin above the waist, the studded straps crossing to the burning chest piece; dark trousers
  and boots.
- **Arms:** long arms hanging past his knees.

**Shackles and chains:** heavy iron shackles on both wrists and both ankles. The wrist chains hang to the ground and
trail behind; the ankle chains drag behind him.

**The cleaver** (take 2, after the user's review: the first was a square plate with the haft through it, held upside
down):
- **The shape:** a butcher's cleaver at his size. A wrapped handle with a pommel and a bolster, then a long
  rectangular blade about 1.6 blocks long and 0.8 tall. The thick spine runs on in line with the handle, the bloody
  edge is on the far side, and the hanging hole is near the tip.
- **Walking:** dragged behind him spine-down, the bloody edge up.
- **The wind-up:** his elbow comes forward and up and the fist sits beside his head, so the cleaver rests back over
  his right shoulder with the edge facing up. The chop comes down edge first. Going from the drag to the wind-up
  keeps the edge on the same side, so the attack is never backwards.

**The head:** as locked: F3's horn boss, buffalo horns, iron nose ring and cyan eye; F2's scowl and slipping
ragged band.

About 120-130 cubes and 17 bones (the arms are now posable). Built in the face study's frame and scaled (0.85) to the planned size; session 2
snaps everything to the texel grid.
