# The Unbound redesign, session 8: shipped

The Horned Frenzy is the real fight now. Generated by `python tools/art/bosses/unbound_ship.py` (after the session
scripts, through `unbound_s7.py`).

## No flickering faces

The ship script refuses to ship a model in which two faces share a plane, since they z-fight in game. The first scan
found clashes: the grate's bars against the fire, the blade against its spine, the shackles against their lips, and
more. The Witch Coven's resolver now runs on The Unbound too, inside the rig's finish step (`unbound_s2.finish`). The
smaller part of each clash moves a fraction of a texel off the plane until nothing clashes (27 small parts moved).
The nudges are found once in the rest pose and kept in each cube's own space, so every pose and every later session
gets the same model; sizes don't change, so the texture layout stays the same. Sessions 2 to 7 were regenerated with
it.

## How the fight uses them (TheUnbound.java)

**The body:** with the Mythic body, the carrier stays unscaled and holds nothing (the model draws him and his
cleaver). Without Mythic, the fight plays as before: a 1.3x vindicator with an iron axe, and vanilla particles.

**The four fixes from the rework plan (boss-rework-plan.md):**
1. **The cleave is locked:** the wedge's origin and facing are fixed when it appears, and he's held still facing it
   through the wind-up. The sweep hits exactly where the warning showed.
2. **Sated cancels the cleave:** an unfinished cleave never lands.
3. **Only his own kill is a devour:** a thrall counts only when he killed it himself with a blow. Thralls the cleave
   cut down, or anything else kills, don't make him sated.
4. **Two separate attacks:** the plain axe swing (vanilla melee, the `swing` clip) and the scripted cleave.

| Server event | Mythic skill | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| spawn, resume, the end of the sated opening | `STAGE_<stage>` | the fire's colour for his health; the flames shown from the burning stage |
| health drops below 2/3 or 1/3 | `ENRAGE_BURNING` / `ENRAGE_INFERNO` | a short roar, the fire flares up into its new stage, a burst of flame and lava; re-tinted 12 ticks later, after any hit flash |
| every 8 s: the cleave | `CLEAVE` (target: the player) | the wind-up through the red wedge, the sweep landing at 1.3 s with the sweep sound |
| every 14 s: the thralls | `CALL`, then the thralls rise 18 ticks later | he crouches and roars; the husks rise on the roar |
| a thrall comes within his reach | `DEVOUR` (target: the thrall) | he lunges and gorges, fist to his mouth (once per thrall) |
| he kills a thrall himself | `SATED` | down on one knee, panting; the fire drops to dull coals and the flames go out for the 3 s opening |
| a melee hit | `SWING` (mob file) | the short chop |
| he's hit | `FLINCH` | a jolt (not during the cleave or the sated opening) |
| always | (the plugin) | embers stream off his chest: a few at first, more when burning, a shower with lava when inferno |
| death | `DEATH` (mob file) | to his knees and folding forward, the fire out; the model hands over to the stand-in so the whole clip plays |

## In game

1. Push the pack repository (`occultech_texture_pack_v1.0`) and the addon repository.
2. Restart your server with the run-server script, so it gets the new jar (pack sha1 `2685388b`).
3. Summon The Unbound at a tier 1 circle, or use `/occultech showcase`.

Please check:
- **Movement:** he drags the cleaver when walking and lifts it over his shoulder to cleave.
- **Fire:** it grows with his stages, and the flames appear below 2/3 health.
- **Sated:** he drops to one knee and the fire dims.
- **Effects:** the chains swing, and the eye burns cyan.
- **Death:** the death plays in full.
