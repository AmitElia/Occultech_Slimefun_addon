# Hollow Warlord, the Headsman: session 1, step 1, take 2 (complete overhaul)

Script: `tools/art/bosses/headsman_take2.py` (run from the repository root; `A B C` to render some takes).
Side by side: [compare.png](compare.png). Take 1 ([s1_silhouettes](../s1_silhouettes/README.md)) was rejected.

User on take 1 (2026-11-18): all three extremely goofy; too lean in the shoulders; the bucket helm goofy; keep the
skulls, but floating above and behind his head (the Rack's position).

What changed for every take:
- **Mass up top:** broad shoulders (about 5 blocks across), heavy upper arms and forearms, the head set low between the
  shoulders and pushed forward, a stoop. The torso narrows in a V to a bare-bone waist (the "hollow"), and his knees bend.
- **Tattered tabard:** narrow strips of different lengths in place of the apron, so his legs show.
- **The taken skulls float:** no spikes. They hang in a wide arc high behind his head, at the same 2x scale as his
  own skull, the side ones tipped, with short soul-fire wisps under each. They float in world space, so they don't lean
  with him (they can bob and drift in animation).
- About 5.5 blocks to the top of the helm, 7.5 to the top skull.

Self-review: A has the strongest shape (broad pauldrons, a clear V, the helm framed between the neck guards). In B's
front view, the bare skull merges with the collarbones and shoulder blades. C's lopsided top reads well from the front
but heavy from the side. The helm and skull are still blockout shapes; they get their own study in step 2.
