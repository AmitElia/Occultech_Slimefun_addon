Picked: take 2's A, the Plate ([s1_take2](../s1_take2/README.md)), with H3, the Kettle Hat, and S1, the Executioner's
sword ([s1_study](../s1_study/README.md)). Script: `tools/art/bosses/headsman_blockout.py <client folder> [pose ...]`.
Sheets: [blockout.png](blockout.png) (every pose, four views), [hero.png](hero.png) (idle and sweep, larger).

- About 5.2 blocks across the pauldrons, 5.5 to the hat, 7.5 to the top floating skull; about 1,150 cubes in the
  blockout (most of them are the one-cube-per-texel skull faces, which become textures in session 4).
- The skulls (his own and the three taken ones) carry the real wither skeleton pixels at 2x, the eyes lit soul cyan.
- Since step 2 the kettle hat's brim slopes down at 26 degrees (it read as a ranger's hat at 16), and a ridge runs
  front to back over the crown, so it reads as a war hat.
