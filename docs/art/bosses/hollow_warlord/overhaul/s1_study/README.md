# Hollow Warlord, the Headsman: session 1, step 2 (helm and sword study)

On take 2's A, the Plate (picked 2026-11-18). Script: `tools/art/bosses/headsman_study.py <client folder>`
(`--helms` or `--swords` renders one half). The skulls carry the real wither skeleton pixels from the 26.2 client at 2x,
one cube per texel, the eyes lit soul cyan. The vanilla face has the eyes on texel row 4 and the teeth on row 6, so at
2x the eyes sit 6-8 units above the skull's underside; every helm is fitted to that row.

Sheets: [helms.png](helms.png), [swords.png](swords.png).

| Helm | What it is |
|---|---|
| H1 Sallet | a low rounded dome, a rolled brow just above the eyes, cheek plates, swept back into a two-lame tail; the face bare |
| H2 Barbute | closed round the whole skull except a T cut over the eyes and down to the teeth |
| H3 Kettle Hat | a high dome with a wide brim sloping down; the lit eyes glow in its shadow; the face bare |

| Sword | What it is |
|---|---|
| S1 Executioner's | broad and square-ended, the end thickened, short quillons turned down |
| S2 Flamberge | a wavy blade, parrying lugs over a leather ricasso |
| S3 Greatsword | long and straight, tapering to a point, a wide guard |

The floating skulls now use the vanilla pixels too: they read as wither skulls straight away.
