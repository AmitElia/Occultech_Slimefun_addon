# Hollow Warlord, the Headsman: session 1, step 1 (silhouettes)

Script: `tools/art/bosses/headsman_silhouettes.py` (run from the repository root; `A B C` to render some takes).
Brief: [plan.md](../plan.md). Side by side: [compare.png](compare.png).

Same body in all three: a lean wither skeleton about 5.5 blocks to the top of the helm, with the vanilla skull at 2x
under a blackstone headsman's helm (soul-cyan slit, the jaw showing), a real ribcage, a gorget, and a leather apron.
The executioner's sword is 4.6 blocks long (square end, crimson brand down the fuller). Each take is shown resting
(sword on the shoulder), walking (dragging it) and sweeping (the hip-height whirl). Colours are blockout zones.

Self-review notes: B's skulls merge with the apron in silhouette and the sweep's sword covers them. A's are clear but sit
at shoulder height, where the guards and the sword cross them. C's stay above everything, so they can be counted from
anywhere in the arena. The helm, skull and sword shapes are placeholders until the step 2 head study.
