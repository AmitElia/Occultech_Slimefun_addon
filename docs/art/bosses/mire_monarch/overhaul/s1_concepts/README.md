# Mire Monarch redesign, session 1: three directions, each in both forms

**The boss:** a giant crowned cellar toad, chosen 2026-10-11. It was renamed from the Gelatinous Sovereign
(`MIRE_MONARCH`), and the fight is unchanged:
- it marks a player and leaps onto them, the belly-flop sending a shockwave players jump;
- at 66% it retches up three spawn that crawl back to the altar to feed it;
- at 33% it dries and splits, leaner and faster, sweating acid puddles.

Each direction is shown big (phase 1) and dried (phase 2).

**Built from vanilla's frog:**
- a flat body, with the head split by a wide dark mouth line;
- two cube eyes standing on top of the head, a dark horizontal pupil in a gold iris;
- a croaking throat sac;
- webbed hands and feet as flat planes with spread toes (vanilla's are flat planes too);
- colours from vanilla's cold, mire-green frog.

Added for the toad: big folded haunches bulging out at the rear sides (its signature shape), a warty back, and a
chunky crown.

The fight's beats on each:
- **The leap:** the haunches coil, which is the telegraph, then the belly-flop sends the shockwave.
- **The 66% spawn:** tadpoles or egg clumps it retches up.
- **The 33% acid:** what runs from its split skin.

**My read:**
- **A** is the most vanilla-frog: you know what it is at a glance.
- **B** is the most aggressive and unusual silhouette: a round mouth with horns.
- **C** is the most regal, but it sits upright between leaps, so its leap needs it to drop forward onto all fours (it
  could do that as its tell).
