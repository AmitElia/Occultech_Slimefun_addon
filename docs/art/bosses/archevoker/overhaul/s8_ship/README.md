# The Archevoker, session 8: shipped

The dark priest on his floating throne is the real fight now, with his two gilded casting hands as his totems. The
models are generated by `python tools/art/bosses/archevoker_ship.py` and the Mythic files by
`python tools/art/bosses/archevoker_mythic_skills.py` (after the session scripts, through `archevoker_s7.py`).

## The fight (Archevoker.java), as agreed in the plan

**The carriers:**
- **The priest:** an evoker with its own mind removed (no fleeing, no vexes, no fangs of its own). The fight glides
  him about 8 blocks from his target and turns him to face it every tick. Invisible under the model, the evoker
  draws nothing.
- **The hands:** two vex carriers with their AI off (each spawned with `spawnLookalike`, so it is an extra mob: no
  drops, removed at the end). The fight flies them every tick.

**The hands are his totems.**
- **Patterns:**
  - The cyan hand draws the line and the ring; the purple hand draws the spiral and the burst.
  - Each pattern's floor warning is in its hand's colour.
  - The priest plays his own gesture (`line`, `ring`, `spiral`, `burst`) as the hand draws.
- **Hard to kill:** each has 110 health, scaled for the group like the boss.
  - They hover 4.5 blocks up, out of a sword's reach, on his right (cyan) and his left (purple), drifting round him.
  - A hit on a hovering hand lands, and the hand darts away and can't be hurt for a second.
- **The opening:** while drawing, a hand swoops down to 1.6 blocks, within reach and clear of the fangs, and holds
  still. That lasts the 1 s warning and half a second after (`tell`, the circle spinning fast).
- **While a hand lives he cheats death once.** At 0 the hands rush in front of his chest, he slumps into them
  (`caught`, a gold-white glow), and he comes back at 40%.
- **Each dead hand makes him stronger.**
  - **With one dead:** he recoils (`hand_lost`), his glow dims, and he casts its patterns himself, faster: every
    4.5 s instead of 6, or 3.5 s instead of 4.5 below half.
  - **With both dead:** he rises off his throne (`rise`, then `risen_idle`) and can't come back.
    - He burns violet and casts two patterns at once every 3 s (2.5 s below half), on two fighters when there are two.
    - The fangs hit for 16 instead of 12 (the new mechanic "Fangs (risen)").
    - His burst comes twice as often.
- **A dead hand** plays its death clip on a stand-in: its eyelid shuts and its light goes out.

**The fixes from the rework plan:**
1. **Cast priority:** a pattern being drawn holds the burst back, and cheating death cancels anything half-drawn.
2. **The burst's warning** is 15 ticks.
3. **No extra damage source:** the fangs stay vanilla and labelled; the clips deal nothing.
4. **No vanilla casts:** the carrier's own spells are gone, and there are no vexes.
5. **Saved with the fight:** which hands live and whether he has revived, so a restart can't hand back a revival
   or a dead hand.

**Also:**
- **Now and then while he waits** he folds his arms (`idle_fold`, now a 4 s gesture that ends by itself).
- **His tints are put right** after each hit flash, as for the Masquerade.

**Without Mythic** the fight runs on a scaled-up evoker with two plain vexes as the hands. The rules are the same.

## Engine changes (for every boss)

- **`BossBehavior.modifyAddDamage`:** a behavior can change or cancel a player's hit on an extra mob (the hands'
  dodge). A cancelled hit earns no share.
- **`BossBehavior.saveData` / `restoreData`:** what a fight has been through that health can't show is saved with
  it. `FightState` carries it as an optional eighth field, so states saved before still read.
- **`Prop.showPart`:** shows a bone that starts hidden (the dead hand's eyelid).

- **Unit tests:** pass, including two new `FightState` tests (behavior data round-trips; an old saved line still

- **Self-test with Mythic and BetterModel (server b):** the first run had one failure, the known flaky crash-recovery

  played fight shows:
  - **the hands:** their flight, the swoop, the dodge and how hard they are to catch;
  - **the timing:** the clips against the warnings;
  - **the late moments:** the catch, the rise and his risen loop, the fold gesture;
  - **the tints and the eyelid.**

## To deploy

1. Push the pack clone (`occultech_texture_pack_v1.0`, sha1 `a14b81c7`).
2. With your server stopped, copy `target/Occultech-0.1.0-SNAPSHOT.jar` into `run/plugins`. A jar with the old pack's
   hash makes the client drop the whole pack.
3. Optional: remove the review rigs from `run/plugins/BetterModel/models`. They are
   `occultech_archevoker_rig.bbmodel` and `occultech_archevoker_hand_cyan_rig.bbmodel` / `_purple_rig.bbmodel`; the
   shipped models replace them.
