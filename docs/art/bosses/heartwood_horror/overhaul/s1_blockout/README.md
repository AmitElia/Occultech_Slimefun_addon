# Heartwood Horror, session 1: blockouts of the combined design

Script: `tools/art/bosses/heartwood_s1.py` (run from the repository root; `A B C` to render some takes, `quick` for a
fast pass). Side by side: [compare.png](compare.png). Plan: [../plan.md](../plan.md).

## The design (picked 2026-11-18 from five pitches)

- **Silhouette:** a hunched trunk on long thin stilt legs (backward-kneed). Long arms grow out of the trunk and drag
  behind it on root fingers. Nothing is symmetric: it is a twisted, grown tree.
- **Chest:** a shell of resin under the hunched trunk. Inside it an older creaking sleeps, curled like a foetus (the
  vanilla creaking's parts at 1.5x: head bowed onto its knees, arms round its shins).
- **Face:** a creaking heart hanging below the trunk's front on a root, like a lantern, caged in roots, its eye glowing.
  It hangs at about 2.5 blocks, just over a player's head.
- **Eyeblossoms** grow all over the arms and the hump, some open (white petals, orange eye), some closed (grey buds).
- **Pale hanging moss** hangs in strands from the arms and the belly, never as a veil.
- About 6.5 blocks to the top of the hump, 7 to 8 blocks across with the arms, 9 to 10 long with the arms trailing.

from three-quarter, front and side (silhouettes) and from a player's eye.

## Self-review

- **A** has the clearest silhouette from the side: the hunch, the lantern dangling in front, the arms trailing behind
  like a train of roots. From the front it reads as a spider made of branches.
- **B** is the most spidery from the front (five arms), but from the side the split trunk stands more upright and the
  arms bunch behind into a tail. The split only reads up close.
- **C**'s crutch arm gives it the most unsettling gait (an ape-like lean onto one huge arm), and the twist makes it
  read crooked from every side. Its front view is the heaviest.
- Every take: the trunk and limbs are still blockout boxes, so close up they look like stacked planks. In session 2
  they become fewer, cleaner chamfered masses. The resin is drawn see-through here only to show the sleeper. Whether it
  can be see-through in game is a session 4 question; if not, the sleeper's face and hands break the surface (as in A).
- **Hitboxes:** the body is tall and the arms are thin, so the hitboxes go on the trunk, the womb and the lantern
  (session 2).

## For the user

Pick a take, or mix (for example A's trunk with C's crutch arm), and list changes.
