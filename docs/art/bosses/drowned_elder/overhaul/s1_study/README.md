# Drowned Elder (the Wyrm), session 1 step 2: the head and segment study

Script: `tools/art/bosses/drowned_study.py`. Plan: [../plan.md](../plan.md). Body plan picked in step 1:
[B, the Wyrm](../s1_serpent/README.md). Colours are blockout zones only (teal = a glowing eye).

## Heads

Each head sheet shows the head **at rest** and in its **stare** (the Mining Fatigue pulse), with two body segments
behind it for context.

| Head | At rest | In the stare |
|---|---|---|
| [H1, the Elder](H1_the_elder.png) | vanilla's elder head unchanged: the box body, four plates, twelve edge spikes, the eye on its face | the eye lights up |
| [H2, the Petal Maw](H2_the_petal_maw.png) | vanilla's four plates have become tapered, hooked jaws hinged at mid-head, closed into a snout over the face | the jaws peel open like petals, with rows of teeth on their inner faces, and the eye stares out of a dark throat |
| [H3, the Spike Hood](H3_the_spike_hood.png) | vanilla's head; its back spikes have grown into a fan of nine long spikes webbed with fin, folded back along the neck | the hood flares into a spiked halo behind the head, like a cobra's |

My pick: **H2**. The opening maw is the boss's signature moment: it turns vanilla's own plates into the weapon, gives
the stare a clear tell, and makes the closed head read as a serpent's snout, not a box. H3's hood would also suit the
dive (flared as it rises).

## Segments

Each segment sheet shows a short run of four segments with the second one's eye **shut** and **open** (the beam tell).

| Segment | Build | How the eye opens |
|---|---|---|
| [S1, the Barrel](S1_the_barrel.png) | vanilla's barrel: a core, four plates, the dorsal spike, small side spikes | the side plate slides back off the eye |
| [S2, the Shingle](S2_the_shingle.png) | the top and side plates tilt back and overlap the next segment like roof shingles (a saw-tooth back), the bottom flush | the side shingle swings out like a gill cover |
| [S3, the Vertebra](S3_the_vertebra.png) | a slimmer core with a raised collar of plates at the front of each segment, its spikes set round the collar, like a spine | a lid slides off the eye between two collars |

My pick: **S3**. The collars give the body rhythm along its whole length (it reads as a spine at any distance), and
the eye sits in the gap between collars where it's easy to spot. S2's lifting gill cover is the clearest tell; it could
be borrowed for S3's eyes.

Not decided here: proportions and spike lengths along the body, the fluke, the pectoral fins (all in step 3, the full
blockout).

**Picked 2026-11-17: H3, the Spike Hood + S2, the Shingle.** Next: step 3, the full blockout ([../s1_blockout](../s1_blockout/README.md)).
