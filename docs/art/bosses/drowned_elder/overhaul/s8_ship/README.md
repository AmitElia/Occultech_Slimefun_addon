## What shipped

- **`src/main/models/occultech_drowned_elder.bbmodel`:** the final rig from the session 7 clips with the session 6 neutral
  eyes: 124 elements, 9 clips, 4 hitboxes. The ship script refuses to ship if any faces would flicker or any texel is
  half-clear.
- **`src/main/mythic/Mobs/DrownedElder.yml`, `Skills/DrownedElder.yml`:** look and sound only. Each skill is
  `DROWNED_ELDER_<ACTION>`: `AIM`, `LOCK` (the three beam eyes go white), `STARE` / `PULSE` / `STARE_END`, `SHED`, `DIVE`,
  `EMERGE`, `FLINCH`, `STATE_CALM` / `STATE_SURGED` (the beam eyes' tints) and `DEATH`.
- **`boss/tier2/DrownedElderRig.java` (generated by the ship script):** where the model's parts are, in blocks from the
  carrier's feet: each beam eye's two sides through the aim clip (11 frames), the head's eye at the stare's pulse, the
  surge's two holes, the body's middle (where guardians shed).
- **`MythicPackInstaller`:** both files and the model are on its fixed lists.

## The fight (`DrownedElder.java`)

Without MythicMobs and BetterModel nothing changes: a 2.5x elder guardian gliding over the land. With the model:

- **Carrier:** an unscaled, invisible, puppeted elder guardian sitting on the floor; the head's centre is about 4 blocks
  over it. It glides after its target as before, but turns at most 1.5 degrees a tick (the whole 18-block body swings
  with it), and keeps its head in line with its body.
- **Clips play from where the carrier stands, so it holds still through each one:** the spawn (while it's still at the
  circle), the beam coil, the stare, the shed and the whole surge. While aiming it may still turn slowly on its target.
- **Multi-beam:** `AIM` starts with the beams' 40-tick charge. Each of the (up to) three beams leaves **its own eye**
  (segments 3, 5 and 7), the side nearer its target, following the eye as the body coils (the rig table, interpolated
  every tick). The three eyes go white (`LOCK`) 15 ticks before the beams fire, when Abyss.Beam turns its beam white
  too; the state tint comes back after they fire.
- **Pressure (Mining Fatigue):** `STARE` coils it overhead with the hood flared and the head's eye glaring; the pulse
  comes on the glare (20 ticks in), with a glow burst at the eye; the eye calms at 44 ticks. If it's busy when the
  pulse is due, the stare waits until it's free.
- **Guardians:** `SHED` sends a shudder down the body; the two guardians appear at the middle of the body 10 ticks in,
  in a burst of bubbles.
- **Tidal Surge (half health):** `DIVE` rears and strikes head first into the floor about 9 blocks ahead; **the waves
  start where its head went in**, the first on the dive's contact (20 ticks). 20 ticks after the last wave starts,
  `EMERGE` pours the arch into the floor while the head bursts out of a second hole next to the carrier (a splash at the
  hole 14 ticks in). From the dive on, the beam eyes are tinted surged (cold blue).
- **Flinch:** on a hit, only between clips and at most once in 15 ticks. **Hit flash:** the tint is put back 12 ticks
  after a hit (white if the eyes are locked).
- **Death:** the corpse stand-in plays the death clip with every eye dark.

Nothing has been fought with a player. Things only a played fight shows:

- **Which side is the model's right.** The rig table assumes Blockbench +x is the model's right. If the beams leave from
  the wrong side of a coiled body (an eye on the far side of the S), mirror `right` in `DrownedElderRig` (one sign).
- **The beam origins** through the coil, the lock at 15 ticks, the waves starting at the dive's hole.
- **The swim-to-attack cut:** an attack clip starts from the swim's first frame; if BetterModel snaps there, the tail
  jumps up to about 2 blocks.
- **The head bone (`hi_head`)** follows the carrier's look; the carrier's pitch is held at 0 so the head stays in line.
  Check the recoil, the lunge and the roar still read.
- **Turning:** at 1.5 degrees a tick the tail sweeps quickly when it turns round; if it reads as a whip, lower it.
- **The four hitboxes** follow the swimming body; check a hit on one counts once.
