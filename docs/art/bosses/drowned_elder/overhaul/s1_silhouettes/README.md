# Drowned Elder, session 1 step 1: three takes on the Lure-Eyed Oracle

Script: `tools/art/bosses/drowned_silhouettes.py`. Each sheet shows **idle**, **aim** (the multi-beam: each lure locked on
its own player) and **surge** (reared up for the Tidal Surge) from three-quarter, front and side silhouettes and a
player's eye. Colours are blockout zones only.

Shared body (vanilla's `GuardianModel` from the 26.2 client, at 4x):
- **The guardian's barrel**, octagonal like vanilla's body with its plates, now tapering into a real fish body: head,
  mid, rear, then the three tail segments and the fin.
- **The face:** vanilla's face plate, with a big **blind milky eye** in a dark socket under a heavy brow.
- **An anglerfish underbite:** the lower jaw juts past the face, slightly open, with irregular needle fangs that are small
  guardian spikes.
- **Twelve spikes** on the body's edges, tapered. The ones underneath are short so they never read as legs.
- **A crown of bleached dead coral fans** (crossed planes, like vanilla coral) and wet sponge over the brow.
- **Tattered pectoral fins** and a dorsal fin.
- It glides about 1.4 blocks over the floor.

| Take | The lures | Size (blocks) |
|---|---|---|
| [A, the Angler](A_the_angler.png) | three lures arch forward over the brow, their eyes hanging in front of the blind face, like a real anglerfish | 7.5 x 6.9 x 14.4 |
| [B, the Candelabra](B_the_candelabra.png) | three tall stalks rise from the back and hook over at the top; the eyes look down from high above everyone | 7.5 x 9.5 x 12.9 |
| [C, the Spine-Seer](C_the_spine_seer.png) | three of its own top spikes have grown into the lures and curve forward: the strongest vanilla tie | 7.5 x 7.5 x 14.4 |

**My pick: C.**
- Its lures come from the guardian's own spikes, which is the most vanilla-rooted idea.
- When aiming, the stalks swing wide to either side, so players see clearly which one is locked on them.
- Its silhouette stays compact, where B's tall stalks dominate.

A is the most classic anglerfish, and B reads best from far away.

## Fixed in self-review

- **The first take was vanilla's body at 4x:** one giant cube on stick-like spikes, read as a crate on legs (the Mire
  Monarch lesson). It's now a tapering octagonal fish body with short spikes underneath.
- **The face read as a toaster.** It has an octagonal socket now, and the upper teeth no longer line up like a grille:
  they're irregular, slanted needle fangs.
