# Drowned Elder, session 1 step 1: the Abyssal Serpent, three body plans

Script: `tools/art/bosses/drowned_serpent.py`. Plan: [../plan.md](../plan.md). Each sheet shows **idle**, **aim** (three
segments open their eyes on the players) and **surge** (the dive: head into the floor, body arched over it) from a
three-quarter view, side and top silhouettes, and a player's eye. Colours are blockout zones only.

Shared: the head is vanilla's Elder Guardian at 3x (the 12x12x16 body, its four plates, the twelve edge spikes, the eye on
its face), 2.25 blocks across; the segments trail behind it, ending in a forked tail fluke. 16-19 blocks long in a
22-block arena; it hovers about 1.2 blocks over the floor.

| Take | Body | How it holds itself | Size (blocks, idle) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A, the Chain](A_the_chain.png) | 8 separate guardian barrels with a gap between them, each with its plates and four diagonal spikes | flat, a sideways S close to the floor (reads best from above) | 6.5 x 4.6 x 19 |
| [B, the Wyrm](B_the_wyrm.png) | 10 overlapping armoured segments, one tall spike per segment along the back, small side spikes, pectoral fins | humps that rise and dip, the classic sea serpent | 7.6 x 7.1 x 17 |
| [C, the Oarfish](C_the_oarfish.png) | 19 tall, narrow ribbon segments without spikes, a continuous crest, five crest rays sweeping back from the head | rears upright, head ~9 blocks up, looking down at you | 5.3 x 14.9 x 9 |

My pick: **B**. It reads as a sea serpent from every side, the dorsal spike row is the guardian's spikes made into a
spine, and the surge arc is the strongest of the three. A is the most "made of guardians" but flat from the side; C is
the most striking idle, but its upright S can read as a seahorse.

Not decided here (step 2, the head and segment study): the head is still vanilla's plain box; the segment eyes, the
jaw/face treatment and the fluke shape are placeholders.

**Picked 2026-11-17: B, the Wyrm.** Next: step 2, the head and segment study ([../s1_study](../s1_study/README.md)).
