# The Abyssal Warden, session 8: shipped

The Lost Helmet is the real fight now. The model and the Mythic files are generated by
`python tools/art/bosses/warden_ship.py`, run after the session scripts (through `warden_s7.py`).

## Shipped

- **The model:** `src/main/models/occultech_abyssal_warden.bbmodel`. It has every clip from session 7, the hitbox, the
  neutral iris and the hidden phase parts. At startup it is installed into BetterModel's models folder, and it is
  merged into the Occultech pack.
  - The ship script refuses a model with two faces on one plane, because those flicker. The bloodshot sclera is left
    out of that check: it is a copy that never shows together with the sclera.
  - It also refuses half-clear texels, because those hide whatever is drawn behind them.
- **Mythic:** `src/main/mythic/Mobs|Skills/AbyssalWarden.yml`, listed by the pack installer.
  - `OCCULTECH_ABYSSAL_WARDEN`: a silent guardian carrier.
  - Cosmetic skills for every clip, tint, part swap and sound.
- **The pack:** sha1 `f235a504`. It merges BetterModel's output for the fifteen shipped models. The pack is copied
  into the pack repository's local clone, and the jar carries the same zip.

## The fight (AbyssalWarden.java)

**The carrier:** a guardian with its own mind removed (no laser of its own, no flopping).
- With the model it stays at normal size and hovers a quarter block up; the model's kelp hangs from it. Without
  Mythic, the vanilla guardian is still scaled to 3 and hovers 1.5 blocks up.
- It glides after the nearest player and faces them.
- **Beams leave the eye:** they start in the model's window (3.8 blocks up, 2 ahead), not at the carrier's eyes.

**Which clip plays when:**

| What happens | Clip | Eye tint | Part swap |
|---|---|---|---|
| a beam starts charging | `beam` (the first target is `@trigger`) | white for the last 15 ticks, until it fires | |
| the lash hits someone | `lash` | | |
| the spike warning (1 s) | `spikes` | | the spray shows at contact and goes 12 ticks later |
| the 40-tick rest | `rest` | resting (dim) | |
| below half | `idle_furious` replaces the idle | furious | `sclera` → `sclera_blood` |
| at a third | `burst`, then `idle_33` replaces both idle and walk | | at 0.8 s: the side window's glass → burst window, and the arm, forearm and hand show |
| the hand smash | `smash` | | |
| a hit (at most every 12 ticks, not while busy) | `flinch` | re-tinted 12 ticks after the last hit (the hit flash) | |
| death | `death` on the corpse | dead | the corpse is given the phase parts it had |

- **From a third,** every clip with a `_33` version plays that version (`beam_33`, `lash_33`, `spikes_33`,
  `rest_33`, `flinch_33`). Those keep the hand where it belongs instead of snapping it to its bind pose.
- **The walk is swapped too:** once the hand is out, `defaultstate` replaces the walk with `idle_33`. He glides all
  the time, and the walk would have stuck the arm out sideways.

**The 33% burst (new):**
- **The burst:** when health drops under a third, the side window bursts (2.4 s). Nothing else starts until it is
  over.
- **The smash:** from then on he smashes every 8 s (10 s solo). He locks a ring on his target (2.5 blocks wide,
  1.25 s warning) and works out where to be so the hand lands on it. The hand comes down 4.2 blocks to his right and
  4.1 ahead (session 7's contact pose), so he turns 45 degrees left of the target and closes in at up to 0.3 blocks a
  tick.
- **The hit:** 32 area damage and a knock-up, only inside the ring. The ring is the truth: if he can't close in in
  time, the hit still lands on the ring and nowhere else. The hand stays down until 2.25 s, an opening to hit him.
- **Without Mythic,** the guardian holds still and the ring is the whole attack.

**The resting fix:** while he rests or bursts, he now holds the way he faced. Before, gliding "towards himself" snapped
his yaw to 0.

**After a restart:** the fight saves `enraged` and `arm`. A resumed fight puts the bloodshot eye, the hand, the burst
window and the phase idles back at once (`PHASE_FURIOUS`, `PHASE_33`), without the clips.
