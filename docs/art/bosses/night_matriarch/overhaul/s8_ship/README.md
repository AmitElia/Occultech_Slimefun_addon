# The Night Matriarch redesign, session 8: shipped

The sculk-infested Phantom Queen is the real fight now. Generated by `python tools/art/bosses/matriarch_ship.py` (after
the session scripts, through `matriarch_s7.py`).

## No flickering faces

The ship script refuses to ship a model in which two faces share a plane, since they z-fight in game. The first scan
found 40 clashes: the overlapping tail feathers, the ribs, the wing spars against their membranes, and two finger
bones. The Witch Coven's resolver now runs on her too, inside the rig's finish step (`matriarch_s2.finish`). The
smaller part of each clash moves a fraction of a texel off the plane (18 parts moved), found once in the rest pose and
kept for every pose. Sessions 2 to 7 were regenerated with it.

## How the fight uses them (NightMatriarch.java)

**The body:** with the Mythic body, the phantom carrier stays unscaled (the model draws her 12 blocks across).
Without Mythic, the fight plays as before on a scale-4 phantom.

**The fixes from the rework plan (boss-rework-plan.md):**
1. **No teleport on a timed-out dive:** before, a dive that ran out of time teleported her onto the mark and hit
   everyone there. Now:
   - she lands only when she has actually reached her mark, within 2 blocks of it;
   - a dive that runs out of time is abandoned: she pulls up and flies on, nothing is hit, and she isn't stunned;
   - so is a dive that stops closing on its mark for 6 ticks (something in the way).
2. **The mark stays until resolution:** the dark circle now stays down through the warning and the dive (30 + 40
   ticks), not just the warning.
3. **A readable rake:** when she sweeps close she lunges (the bite clip and a swoop sound). The bite lands 6 ticks
   later on the nearest player still in reach, so a step back dodges it. It no longer happens during the tell or the
   dive.
4. **Free flight only after the full recovery:** unchanged; she takes off when the 1.5 s stun ends.

| Server event | Mythic skill | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| spawn, the stun's end, a pulled-up dive, day or night changing | `STATE_DAY` / `STATE_NIGHT` | the sculk's glow: dimmed by day, full at night |
| the dive's warning (the dark circle) | `TELL` (target: the player) | she rears, wings raised, horns flared; the glow flares; a swoop and a shriek |
| the dive starts | `DIVE` | wings swept back, nose down |
| she reaches the mark | `IMPACT` | slammed down, sprawled and twitching for the stun; the glow gutters; a burst of sculk |
| the stun ends, or the dive is abandoned | `TAKEOFF` | a great downbeat back into the air |
| every 12 s: the swarm | `CALL` | head up, wings spread, a screech; the phantoms join |
| she sweeps close to a player | `BITE` (target: the player) | the head lunges down; the bite lands 6 ticks later |
| she's hit | `FLINCH` | a jolt (not while stunned or diving) |
| always | (the plugin) | sculk sparks shed from her as she flies, more at night |
| death | `DEATH` (mob file) | crashing onto her side, a wing folding over her, the glow out; the model hands over to the stand-in so the whole clip plays |

## In game

1. Push the pack repository (`occultech_texture_pack_v1.0`) and the addon repository.
2. Restart your server with the run-server script, so it gets the new jar (pack sha1 `6fea7529`).
3. Summon the Night Matriarch at a tier 1 circle, or use `/occultech showcase`.

Please check:
- **Flight:** she circles with her wingbeats and banking; the horns flick.
- **The dive:** the tell flare, the dive, the impact and the stun, then the takeoff.
- **Day and night:** the glow is dimmed by day and full at night.
- **The rake:** you can see the lunge coming and step out of it.
- **Death:** the death plays in full.
