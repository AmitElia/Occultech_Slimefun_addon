# Volley redesign, session 8: shipped

The Amethyst Crone and his wards are the real fight now. Generated by `python tools/art/bosses/volley_ship.py`
(after the session scripts).

## Fixes from the first in-game review

- **Gliding while shooting:** BetterModel pins every bone a clip doesn't key to the rest pose unless the clip has
  `override` on, which reads the opposite way round. The action clips (burst, shot, wards, stagger, cracked; the
  ward's hit) now have it on, so the walk keeps the legs moving under them.
- **Invisible wards:** a BetterModel model placed at a location is shown to no one by itself. The ward prop is
  spawned for players within 48 blocks when it appears, and for anyone who comes near on each fight step.
- **Neck texture:** z-fighting. The dark ribcage core, the gorget and the collarbones had their tops on one plane. A
  scan (`bosskit/checks.py`, now run by the ship script, which refuses to ship a model with any) also found:
  - the hump's stacked slabs;
  - the geode rim against its calcite;
  - the elbow against the windlass drum;
  - the prod limbs against their nocks;
  - the heels against the toes.

  All are separated now, with no same-plane overlaps left.
- **The white bit in the right eye:** the eye glint stood half a texel proud and was painted near-white. It now sits
  in the socket with a 0.1-texel lip, in violet.



- `src/main/models/occultech_volley.bbmodel`: the painted rig with its hitbox, variants and nine clips (idle, walk,
  spawn, burst, shot, wards, stagger, cracked, death). The held test poses stay in the review model only.
- `src/main/models/occultech_volley_ward.bbmodel`: the geode-spire ward.
- Both are installed into BetterModel's models folder at startup (staff edits are kept) and merged into the Occultech
  resource pack.

## Who triggers what

| Server event (Volley.java) | Mythic skill | Model |
|---|---|---|
| spawn | `model`, `bodyrotation` on the mob | spawn clip (automatic) |
| burst warning | `VOLLEY_BURST_PREPARE` | `burst`: crank and load in the 1 s warning, five shots at 1.0-2.0 s |
| every 2 s outside a burst (new) | `VOLLEY_SHOT` | `shot`: load, raise, fire, with the arrow at 0.5 s |
| wards rise | `VOLLEY_WARDS_PREPARE` | `wards` clip; the hump crystals at full brightness |
| the last ward breaks (new) | `VOLLEY_WARDS_BROKEN` | `stagger` clip; the crystals back to world light (the glowing shards stay full-bright) |
| below 40% health (new) | `VOLLEY_CRACKED` | `cracked` clip; the big shards swap for the snapped stumps |
| death | - | death clip (automatic) |

Each ward is a BetterModel model at its spot: packets only, so nothing is saved and a crash leaves nothing behind.
- It plays `rise` when it appears.
- Hits 1-3 each hide a ring shard and play `hit`; the 4th plays `shatter`, then the model goes.
- Its hitbox is now 1.4 x 2 blocks, matching the model; the vanilla look keeps the old 1.26-block box.
- The beam runs from the ward's core tip to the geode on Volley's hump.

## Arrows leave the arbalest

- **Every arrow is fired by Volley.java:** the five-arrow burst and the new snap shots.
- **The Mythic body has no vanilla bow AI:** before, the skeleton's own bow shots came out of its eye, inside the
  model's chest.
- **Snap shots replace those vanilla shots:** one every 2 s outside a burst, the same "Arrow" attack and damage, so
  the balance holds.
- **Launch point:** the arbalest's muzzle in the aim pose, measured from the model ([muzzle.json](muzzle.json): 1.2
  blocks to his right, 2.9 up, 5.2 ahead). It turns with Volley, and he turns to face his target as he fires.
- **Close targets:** the launch point slides back along the barrel towards his shoulder, so an arrow never starts
  beside or behind a player. If the muzzle is inside a block, it falls back to his eye.
- **Vanilla body:** unchanged (eye, bow AI).

## Tests

- **Self-test with Mythic:** 532 passed, 0 failed on a clean world. New checks: the ward model draws the wards; the
  model, its tracker and its hitbox attach.
- **Vanilla:** 525 passed, 0 failed.
- **Flaky test:** the crash-recovery ritual test failed on the long-used test world and passed after the documented
  world reset; that is the known storage race, not this change.
- **Pack:** BetterModel's output for exactly the two shipped models was merged into the Occultech pack
  (sha1 `604d7cb2`). The jar carries the same zip, so the hash the server sends matches.

## Later fixes (2026-10-11)

- **The eye glint and jaw didn't follow the head:** BetterModel's `h` tag turns only the head bone; the shipped model
  now uses `hi_head` (head with children), so everything on the head turns with it.
- **The plain skeleton showed for a second before the model:** the model is now attached the moment the carrier
  spawns, and the vanilla body is hidden at once.
