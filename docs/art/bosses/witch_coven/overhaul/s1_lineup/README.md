# Witch Coven redesign, step 1: the line-up

The direction and the user's decisions are in [../plan.md](../plan.md). The three sisters share one body (vanilla
witch/villager build, hunched, larger than a player, hovering low, sleeves folded with no hands showing, a real witch's
hat) and differ only by their vessels:
- **the Root-Mother:** a cauldron hanging in front;
- **the Briar:** a censer swung on a chain;
- **the Crook:** a pot on her back with a ladle over her shoulder.

Each sister is shown in a different role colour: green healer, purple curser, red bomber. In the fight every sister
takes every role, and only the colour changes.

![Three takes on the shared body](lineup.png)

| | Take | The body |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Villager hunch** | Vanilla's proportions grown about 1.45x, the back rounded into a hump, the head pushed forward. The closest to vanilla. |
| B | **Deep crone** | Bent much further: the head hangs low in front, so from the front the hump rises round it like a vulture's shoulders. The most menacing at a player's height. |
| C | **Root bell** | A's hunch over a robe flaring into a wide bell, ragged roots trailing behind as it hovers. The most "hedge". |

All three are about 2.75 blocks to the shoulders and 4.4 blocks to the hat's tip; the player is 1.8. Faces are
placeholders (the vanilla face layout); the face is settled in the character study (step 2).
