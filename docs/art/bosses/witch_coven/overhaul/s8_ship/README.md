# Witch Coven redesign, session 8: shipped

The hedge witches' coven is the real fight now. Generated by `python tools/art/bosses/coven_ship.py` (after the
session scripts, through `coven_e2.py`).

## No flickering faces

The ship script refuses to ship a model in which two faces share a plane: they z-fight in game. The first scan
found 53, 15 and 10 clashing pairs, mostly where the cauldron's rope segments meet at the corners. Brood Mother's
resolver now runs on the coven too (`coven_s2.separate`): the smaller part of each clash moves a fraction of a texel
off the plane until nothing clashes (19, 7 and 6 small parts moved). The nudges are found once per sister in her
rest pose and kept in each cube's own space, so every pose and every later session gets the same model. Sizes don't
change, so the texture layout stays the same. Sessions 2 to 7 and E2 were regenerated with it.

## How the fight uses them (WitchCoven.java)

- **Three bodies in one fight:** `BossFight.spawnBoss(part, ...)` spawns a main boss with a body of its own. Its
  Mythic carrier is `OCCULTECH_<BOSS>_<PART>` and its model `occultech_<boss>_<part>`. Each sister's actions run
  `WITCH_COVEN_<SISTER>_<ACTION>`.
- **The fixes from the plan (boss-rework-plan.md):**
  - **The curse is dodgeable:** the mark is the ground the player stood on, and only players still on it when it
    lands (1 s later) are cursed. Before, it followed the player.
  - **Casts are dropped** when a witch dies or loses her role before they land.
  - **Wind-ups take real time:** each clip starts early enough that its release lands on the real moment.

| Server event | Lead | Mythic skill | What you see |
|---|---|---|---|
| spawn | - | `model` on the mob; `IS_<ROLE>` | the spawn clip; her brew in her role colour at once |
| the roles rotate (every 15 s) | 10 ticks | `BECOMES_<ROLE>` | she strikes her vessel; her brew takes the new colour on the strike, and again 12 ticks later (after any hit flash); a streak of the new colours runs from sister to sister |

| heal (every 4 s) | 15 ticks | `HEAL` (target: the sister) | the heal clip; at the pour a stream of green motes reaches her sister, then the heal lands with a green bubble burst |
| curse (every 5 s) | the 20-tick warning | `CURSE` (target: the player) | the curse clip through the warning; then the cauldron's spill, the bottle's smashing shards and purple droplets, or the pot's puddle on the mark |
| throw (every 4 s) | 15 ticks | `THROW` (target: the player) | the throw clip; the potion flies as her throwable (the glob, the bottle, the wave, dyed red); where it breaks: droplets, glass shards or a wide splash |
| she's hit | - | `FLINCH` | the flinch, layered over whatever she's doing |
| now and then when idle | - | `CACKLE` | the cackle |
| a sister dies | - | `DEATH` (her mob's `~onDeath`), `GLANCE` | her brew goes spent grey, it spills, her hat drops beside her; the others turn and look (never mid-attack) |
| every second | - | (the plugin) | a bubble pops above each vessel in its role colour |
| every 2 s | - | `HOVER` (her mob's timer) | a few motes of ash under her hem |

**Without Mythic** (vanilla bodies), the fight still works as before: the role shows as an aura over each witch's
head, and the actions play their plain particles and sounds.

## Fixes after the first in-game review

- **The cauldron's liquid came loose in some clips.** The brew bone also holds the drips down the vessel's sides and
  the steam above it, so the clips' brew scale (up to 1.1 wide, 1.3 tall) and the curse's 28 degree tilt pulled the
  drips off the walls and pushed the liquid through them. The bottles had the same problem: one bone, one pivot, so
  their liquid slid out of the glass. The brew no longer moves on its own in any clip (`coven_e2.clips`); the
  sloshing is left to the bubble and splash effects.
- **The bottle witch sometimes lost her robe.** Not reproduced: a server without a player can't show it, and her robe's
  model is identical to the other two. Her one difference was the held bottle shrinking to 1% scale while she reaches
  for the belt, so it now slides up into her sleeve at 85% instead (no near-zero scale anywhere). If the robe still
  vanishes, note what she was doing.
- **The death and the sisters' glance didn't show.** The game removes a dead mob after a second, and BetterModel
  removes its model with it, cutting the 2.4 s death clip. A dying boss's model now hands over to a stand-in at the same
  spot (`BossBody.corpse`) that plays the whole clip, lies still a moment and goes in a puff of smoke after 4.5 s
  (the dead sister's brew spent grey). This applies to every boss with a model, so Volley's and the Brood Mother's
  death clips play in full too. The glance only played if a sister happened to be idle, and her AI turned her away
  at once: now every survivor glances as soon as her cast is done (within 3 s), and is held facing the body while it
  plays. The clip is deeper (a bow, a sleeve to her chest) with a low sigh.

The pack doesn't change (animations live on the server), so its sha1 stays `f6b941c6`. Self-test 547/0.

1. Push the pack repository (`occultech_texture_pack_v1.0`) and the addon repository.
2. Restart your server with the run-server script, so it gets the new jar (its pack hash is `f6b941c6`).
3. Summon the coven at a tier 0 circle, or use `/occultech showcase`.

Please check:
- **Models:** the three sisters appear, each with her vessel.
- **Brew colours:** each brew is in its role colour, and changes on the rotation strike.
- **Actions:**
  - the heal stream reaches the healed sister;
  - the curse mark can be dodged;
  - each witch's throwable flies and breaks in its own way.
- **Personality:** the flinch, cackle and glance play.
- **Death:** a dead sister's hat drops and her brew goes grey.
