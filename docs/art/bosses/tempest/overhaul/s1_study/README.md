# Tempest, session 1 step 2: the head and core study

The user picked **C, the Frozen Blast, with W3, the Full Storm** (2026-11-17). This step settles the face and the heart.
Script: `tools/art/bosses/tempest_study.py`; sheet: [study.png](study.png) (three-quarter, and from below as a player
sees it).

What vanilla gives us (26.2 client): the breeze's face *is* its visor band. Its eyes are two white strokes that start on
the band's sides and step down onto the front towards the middle, an angry V that wraps the corners. Every head keeps
that exactly at 4x. The heavy core's faces are a raised frame round a dark recess; here the recess glows teal.

| | Option | What it adds |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Vanilla Fury | nothing: the vanilla head and its wraparound V eyes at 4x. Cleanest, most recognisable |
| H2 | Seal Circlet | a cracked ring of the seal's stone round the crown, tilted down at the front, glyphs lit, a chunk drifting off the break: the head is bound by the same seal |
| H3 | Gale Crest | the band streams off the back of the head as wind ribbons, with a swept crest on top: the most motion. Risk: it can read as a winged helmet |
| K1 | Vanilla Keystone | the heavy core on its corner, a raised frame and a glowing recess on every face |
| K2 | Seal Setting | the keystone in a broken stone collar (the seal's inner ring) cupping its lower corner. Risk: from the side it reads a little like a pot |
| K3 | Cracked Heart | the core split into eight blocks with seams of light; its top tip blown off and floating beside the head, the light pouring up into the face. Gives the spent pose something to show (the seams go dark) |

My pick: **H2 + K3**. The circlet ties the head to the seal (it was sealed too), and the cracked heart makes the core
the obvious damage target and the source of the light. Mixing is open, such as H3's ribbons only in the burst clip.
