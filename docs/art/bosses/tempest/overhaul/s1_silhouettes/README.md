# Tempest, session 1 step 1: three takes on the Shattered Seal

Script: `tools/art/bosses/tempest_silhouettes.py`. Each sheet shows idle, **locked** (the gust volley: the seal snaps
together) and **spent** (after the burst: shards dropped, core dim, head bowed) from three-quarter, front and side
silhouettes and a player's eye. Colours are blockout zones only.

Shared by all three:
- **Vanilla breeze head at 4x** (BreezeModel from the 26.2 client: 8x8x8 head, 10x3x4 visor band), eyes cut to narrow slits.
- **Heavy core at 3x, stood on its corner** like a keystone gem; the head sits on its top point, so head, core and wind
  read as one creature instead of two stacked crates. Glowing teal faces (tier 2's sea glow).
- **Wind body:** three spiral bands on a funnel that cups the core from below, three breeze rods at 3x tilted 22.5 like
  vanilla.
- **The seal:** pale carved stone, a raised rim, a ring of glyphs between an inner and an outer row; every break is jagged.

| Take | The seal | Size (blocks) | Reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| [A, the Broken Halo](A_the_broken_halo.png) | upright behind the core, five shards drifting a little apart | 6.4 x 6.3 x 2.5 | a cracked holy wheel; the strongest front silhouette; spent: shards slump to the floor round the funnel |
| [B, the Lifted Circle](B_the_lifted_circle.png) | flat round the core's waist, five shards tilted like plates on the wind | 6.5 x 6.3 x 6.4 | the ritual circle itself ripped off the floor; from below players see the glyph underside; spent: it droops like a broken parasol |
| [C, the Frozen Blast](C_the_frozen_blast.png) | seven shards thrown far out, frozen mid-explosion | 8.4 x 8.1 x 4.0 | the most violent and the largest; locked: they slam back into a whole seal from far away (the biggest motion of the three) |

My pick: **B**. It's the summoning circle itself, it reads from every side (A is flat from the side), and the
underside players look at is where the glyphs show best. A is the cleanest front shape, C the most dramatic motion.

Mixes are fine (B's flat circle with C's wild drift when idle, for example).
