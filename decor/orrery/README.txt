Orrery (MythicMobs 5.13 + BetterModel 3.5, no other plugins)

A sun one block across with the solar system turning around it, about 9x9 blocks: the eight planets on oval orbits,
Earth's moon, Saturn's ring, a comet now and then and shooting stars. A decoration only: it does nothing else.

Install: there is no plugin jar; copy these files into the MythicMobs and BetterModel folders already on the server:
  plugins/MythicMobs/Packs/Orrery/packinfo.yml
  plugins/MythicMobs/Packs/Orrery/Mobs/Orrery.yml
  plugins/MythicMobs/Packs/Orrery/Skills/Orrery.yml
  plugins/BetterModel/models/orrery.bbmodel          (the sun and the planets)
  plugins/BetterModel/models/orrery_meteors.bbmodel  (the shooting stars)
  plugins/BetterModel/models/orrery_comet.bbmodel    (the comet)
then run /bm reload (rebuilds the resource pack with the models) and /mm reload, and send players the new resource
pack. Without it every part shows as a purple-black cube.

Updating from the first version: Orreries placed before this update can't be picked up (their carrier was an
armour stand, which ignores punches). Remove them with /mm mobs kill ORRERY (removes every Orrery) and place them
again with fresh eggs.

Get one:  /mm eggs give <player> ORRERY
Place:    use the egg on a block; the sun floats in the space above it (its centre 0.75 above the block), the
          planets turn in a flat disc at the sun's height, out to 4.5 blocks each way. Keep that area clear: the
          planets pass through anything in it.
Pick up:  punch the sun (like breaking a block), or sneak and right-click it; you get the egg back. Anyone can
          do this (it isn't protected by WorldGuard or claim plugins: it is a mob, not a block). Nothing else
          hurts it: fire, lava, explosions and mobs do nothing.
Remove:   /mm mobs kill ORRERY  (/kill doesn't work: the Orrery cancels all damage)

What it is: one frozen cow (no AI, no gravity, silent) wearing three BetterModel models; BetterModel hides the cow.
The planets are one 4-minute loop (every orbit in one clip: BetterModel plays one clip per model at a time). Each
orbit is an ellipse with the sun at a focus, the planet speeding up near the sun (Kepler's second law), and the
periods follow Kepler's third law on the model's own distances (Mercury 3.2 s ... Neptune 30 s; each rounded so it
fits the loop a whole number of times). The sun always faces the viewer and every part is lit fully, day or night. Shooting stars fly about every 3 s on
average (six paths, picked at random); the comet passes about every minute and a half.

Known quirks:
- It survives restarts and chunk unloads (Mythic keeps the mob, BetterModel rebuilds the models and plays the
  planets' loop by itself; the skills put the rest back within 2 s). The planets start their loop again from the
  beginning when that happens.
- Entity clearers: the sun's click box is a BetterModel hitbox (an armour stand and an interaction). The Orrery tags
  them and itself CLE_PROTECTED (ClearLaggEnhanced spares that tag) on spawn, on load and every minute; with another
  clearing plugin, exclude that tag or those entity types, or it can't be picked up after a clear.

Tuning (Mobs/Orrery.yml): the ~onTimer lines for the shooting stars (every 30 ticks, chance 0.45) and the comet
(every 200 ticks, chance 0.25). The models are generated by tools/art/decor/orrery.py in the Occultech repository.
